A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Good.

What exactly is an AI agent?

Think of an agent as a helper that can work through a series of steps toward an outcome. You give it a goal and useful context. It makes a plan, works through the task, and brings the result back for your review.

How is Otto different from a chatbot?

The Otto experience is designed around getting work from thought to outcome: understanding context, planning steps, and preparing something useful. Try Research, Create, or Coordinate above to explore the flow.

Will Otto take actions without asking?

You're in charge. The product is designed around reviewing important actions before they happen. This interactive preview runs locally and never connects to your accounts, sends messages, or changes external tools.

Can I try it before choosing a plan?

Absolutely. Choose “Find your flow” to personalize a workspace preview, or run one of the sample workflows above. The preview needs no sign-up, API key, or payment details.

Can I use it with my team?

The Team plan illustrates a shared workspace for agents, context, and activity. Select “Bring your people” to try that setup. Live collaboration and connected accounts would be part of a production service.
